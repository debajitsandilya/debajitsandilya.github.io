Wireless Battery Management System with Data-Driven Modeling

Designed a wireless BMS architecture for smart buildings using distributed sensors, data-driven ML models, and multi-agent coordination to monitor battery health, predict failures, and optimize energy use. Research conducted at the University of Colorado Denver. Demonstrates how decentralized AI architectures enhance reliability in critical physical infrastructure — with direct applications to renewable energy integration and carbon intensity reduction.

Project 02 · Upcoming Publication · Under Review · CU Denver · Irrometer Inc.

Data-Driven Multi-Agent Framework for Soil Moisture Forecasting & Intelligent Irrigation Decision Support

Developed a data-driven multi-agent AI system for soil-moisture forecasting and intelligent irrigation scheduling — grounded in real-world experience at Irrometer Inc., Riverside, CA, a leading U.S. precision agriculture technology firm. The framework deploys distributed IoT sensors and ML forecasting models to recommend irrigation decisions in real time. Analogous deployments have saved tens of millions of cubic feet of water in individual river basins.
